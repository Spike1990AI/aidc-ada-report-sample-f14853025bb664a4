What this check did not test

An automated check finds only part of what can go wrong. This one did not cover:

  • using the whole site by keyboard alone, start to finish, including menus and checkout
  • how a screen reader reads each page in practice
  • captions and descriptions for video and audio
  • pages we did not sample, and anything behind a login
  • whether the words and images make sense to every shopper

How the score and the risk level are worked out

Accessibility score (0 to 100). Start at 100. For each kind of issue found, take off points by how serious it is: High 12 (the fourth and later High kinds take 6 each, so a long list still moves when you fix things), Medium 5, Low 2. Add a little more for issues that appear in many places (up to 3 for each kind, 12 in all). Best-practice items take off 1 each (at most 6 in total). The lowest possible score is 5. “After the top 3 fixes” is the same sum with those three issues removed.

Risk level follows the score. Under 70 = High, 70 to 89 = Medium, 90 or more = Low. Only issues our check can confirm are counted; anything marked “needs a human look” is left out.
